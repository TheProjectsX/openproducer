## SUMMARY
Leonardo.ai's guide on prompt engineering for Veo 3/3.1 on their platform. Focuses on treating prompting like film directing. Key emphasis on prompt length sweet spot, camera verbs, audio integration, and Start/End frame workflows.

## KEY DATA

### Mindset Shift
- Think like a FILM DIRECTOR, not an image prompt writer
- Veo 3.1 relies on strict prompt adherence, physics simulation, multi-modal capabilities

### Ideal Prompt Length & Structure
- AVOID: overly brief keyword-stuffed lines ("cinematic, 4k, cyberpunk girl")
- AVOID: multi-paragraph novels
- SWEET SPOT: 3-6 sentences, ~100-150 words
- "The Director's Formula":
  1. Subject & Action — who/what is moving, what are they doing
  2. Camera Framing & Movement — how is the shot framed, how does the lens move
  3. Lighting & Atmosphere — time of day, mood, shadow play, weather
  4. Audio Cues — naturally weave in sound descriptions

### Camera & Physics Motion
- Use PROFESSIONAL CINEMATOGRAPHY TERMS, not vague descriptions
- BAD: "camera moves around"
- GOOD: "A slow dolly-in shot toward...", "A sweeping drone pan revealing...", "A handheld tracking shot walking alongside..."
- Describe real-world physics explicitly: "Water ripples at ankle-height", "dust particles swirl in shafts of light", "heavy ceramic mug slams onto wooden table, sloshing hot coffee"

### Native Audio Prompting
- Veo 3.1 generates synchronized audio from text prompts
- DON'T treat audio as afterthought — embed directly into narrative flow
- Detail foreground vs background sounds (depth cues)
- Example: "The camera floats slowly through a flooded hallway. Water ripples at ankle-height... Dripping echoes softly with every step, each droplet landing like a pin on the glassy surface. Ahead, a rusted elevator door creaks open with a loud metallic groan."

### Start & End Frames
- Upload or generate exact first frame and final frame
- Text prompt should describe the TRANSITION between those two anchors
- Focus on pacing of transformation/movement for smooth bridging

### Character/Style Consistency
- Use Leonardo's native image generators or reference tools to lock down look first
- Feed those assets into Veo 3.1 via Image-to-Video (I2V) workflows
- Use descriptive text to dictate HOW that specific image should move

### Pro-Tip Checklist
- [ ] Is it descriptive enough? 3-6 sentences with context, subject, environment
- [ ] Is the camera defined? Clear camera angles and motion paths
- [ ] Is sound integrated? Audio cues woven into scene description
- [ ] Are physics accounted for? Weight, lighting changes, environmental reactions
