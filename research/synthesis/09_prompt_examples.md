He stands on an exterior catwalk in a rain-drenched futuristic megacity at night, illuminated by flickering holographic cyan billboards and wet reflections on dark metallic grating. ## 7. Complete 3-Shot Narrative Sequence (Character Consistency Demo)

**Master Character Description:**  
`"Aria, a 24-year-old solo pilot with braided dark hair, a smudge of black grease across her right cheekbone, wearing a heavy khaki flight jumpsuit with faded orange flight patches and brass utility buckles."`

### Shot 01: Establishing / Cockpit Preparation (8s)
```text
Wide interior shot of Aria, a 24-year-old solo pilot with braided dark hair, a smudge of black grease across her right cheekbone, wearing a heavy khaki flight jumpsuit with faded orange flight patches and brass utility buckles, sitting inside the cramped cockpit of a retro-futuristic cargo shuttle. She systematically flips a row of overhead toggle switches as amber instrument lights glow across the console. Soft volumetric cockpit backlighting, 28mm wide lens. Locked-off static camera framing from passenger side. Audio: SFX: rhythmic heavy switch clicks, turbine whining up to power; Ambience: deep pressurized cockpit hum; Dialogue: None; Music: None.
```

### Shot 02: Medium Action / Throttle Engagement (6s)
```text
Medium shot of Aria, a 24-year-old solo pilot with braided dark hair, a smudge of black grease across her right cheekbone, wearing a heavy khaki flight jumpsuit with faded orange flight patches and brass utility buckles, gripping the dual mechanical flight throttles with both gloved hands and shoving them firmly forward. The cockpit rattles with intense structural vibration as green warning lights reflect on her face. Handheld camera with subtle kinetic shake. Audio: SFX: roaring thruster acceleration, cockpit metal rattling, Aria's sharp intake of breath; Ambience: vibrating cockpit atmosphere; Dialogue: None; Music: None.
```

### Shot 03: Close-Up Performance / Breakthrough (6s)
```text
Medium close-up clean single of Aria, a 24-year-old solo pilot with braided dark hair, a smudge of black grease across her right cheekbone, wearing a heavy khaki flight jumpsuit with faded orange flight patches and brass utility buckles, looking through the cockpit windshield as bright blue starlight breaks across her face. A wide, breathless smile forms as she speaks with relaxed jaw and subtle lip articulation, saying with ecstatic relief: "We broke orbit." Camera slowly pushes in toward her eyes. 50mm prime lens, clean starlight illumination. Audio: SFX: thruster roar softening into a smooth deep engine purr; Ambience: cosmic cabin stillness; Dialogue: Aria says with ecstatic relief: "We broke orbit."; Music: None.
```
