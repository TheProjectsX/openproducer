# 📖 Story Template: True Crime & Cold Open Mystery
> Atmospheric suspense narrative preset. Strictly excludes video cinematography and episode plot.

## 🪝 Hook & Pacing Structure
- **0s – 8s (The Cold Open):** Jarring, unsettling sensory detail or haunting question. Ground time and location immediately.
- **8s – 30s (The Broken Equilibrium):** Contrast mundane normalcy with the eerie arrival of the disturbance.
- **30s – 50s (The Clue / Escalation):** Reveal the inexplicable evidence or chilling testimony.
- **50s – 60s (The Unresolved Outro):** Leave the mystery open, ending on an ominous lingering question.

## 🗣️ Dialogue & Vocal Rules
- **Sentence Cadence:** Measured, deliberate pacing with weighted pauses.
- **TTS Expression Tags:** Inject `<breath>`, `<heavy breath>`, `<short pause>`, `<long pause>`, `<whispers>` at pivotal shifts.
- **Banned Cliches:** "Shocking true story", "You won't believe what happened", "In conclusion".
