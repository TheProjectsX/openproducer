# 📖 Story Template: Stickman Sarcastic Rant
> High-velocity comedy rant preset. Strictly excludes video cinematography and episode plot.

## 🪝 Hook & Pacing Structure
- **0s – 5s (The Hook):** Open immediately with an unhinged, absurd premise or bold hot take. Zero throat-clearing.
- **5s – 35s (The Escalation):** Three rapid escalating micro-frustrations. Accelerate speaking tempo.
- **35s – 45s (The Climax):** Total breakdown of logic, animated panic, or existential realization.
- **45s – 60s (Outro & Punchline):** Abrupt, dry parting remark.

## 🗣️ Dialogue & Vocal Rules
- **Sentence Cadence:** Short, snappy, staccato sentences (maximum 12 words per sentence).
- **TTS Expression Tags:** Inject `<sigh>`, `<pant>`, `<gasp>`, `<laughter>`, `<shout>` at emotional peaks.
- **Banned Cliches:** "Hey guys welcome back", "In this video", "Without further ado", "Don't forget to like and subscribe".
