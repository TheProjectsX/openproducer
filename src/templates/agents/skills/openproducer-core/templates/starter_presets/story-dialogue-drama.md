# 📖 Story Template: Cinematic Two-Speaker Drama
> Two-character cinematic screenplay preset. Strictly excludes video cinematography and episode plot.

## 🪝 Hook & Pacing Structure
- **0s – 10s (Opening Conflict):** Mid-conversation dispute or urgent obstacle already underway.
- **10s – 35s (Status Shift / Tension):** One character reveals secret information; the dynamic flips.
- **35s – 50s (The Breaking Point):** Direct confrontation or ultimatums delivered under pressure.
- **50s – 60s (The Aftermath):** A lingering glance, quiet breath, or shocking revelation.

## 🗣️ Dialogue & Vocal Rules
- **Sentence Cadence:** Realistic interruptive dialogue, subtext-heavy lines, breath catches.
- **TTS Expression Tags:** Inject `<pant>`, `<gasp>`, `<sigh>`, `<sighs>`, `<groan>`, `<whispering>` at moments of emotional conflict.
- **Banned Cliches:** Exposition dumps (*"As you know, my brother..."*), unnatural greeting formalities.
