# Viral Short-Form Mobile Packaging Rules

## 1. Title / Caption Hook Rules
- Keep the title or caption hook under 50 characters so it is instantly readable before truncation.
- Focus on fast emotional friction, immediate mistake callouts, or curiosity gaps.
- Example: `"The secret nobody tells you about Station 9"`

## 2. Cover & First Frame Thumbnail Rules
- 9:16 vertical aspect ratio.
- Keep the bottom 25% completely empty of focal points and text (covered by username, caption, sound pill, and buttons).
- Keep the right 15% edge clear of critical elements (blocked by like/comment icons).
- Subject should be centered or pushed slightly left.
- In-prompt text should be 2 to 3 words max in bold high-contrast font centered above the bottom UI safe line.

## 3. Caption & Description Layout
1. **The 1-Line Hook:** First line creates an irresistible reason to watch to the end.
2. **Hashtags:** Exactly 3 to 4 targeted tags (1 broad, 2 niche, 1 format).
3. **Audio & Tool Attribution:** Brief credit for voice/AI generation.
