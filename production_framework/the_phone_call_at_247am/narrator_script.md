It was 2:47 AM when Rakib’s phone began to ring. <short pause>
On the screen, one name flashed: MOM.
The problem? <short pause> His mother had been dead for three years.

With trembling hands, he answered.
A cracked voice came through the receiver:
<whisper> "Son… don’t open the door. I’m standing outside." </whisper>

Suddenly—three heavy knocks shook the front door. <short pause>
Rakib rushed to the peephole.
The hallway was completely empty.

Then, his phone buzzed. A message from his sister—who lived three hours away:
<whisper> "Brother, I’m standing outside your room. Please don’t open the door!" </whisper>

Rakib froze.
He turned toward the window.
Outside the glass stood a tall, faceless figure in the dark. <short pause>
Slowly, it tilted its head.

The lights snapped off.
In the pitch-black silence, the door latch began to turn. <short pause>
Click… click… click.

The next morning, Rakib was gone.
On his bed, his phone was still recording. <short pause>
A voice whispered:
<whisper> "I’m right behind you, son." </whisper>
