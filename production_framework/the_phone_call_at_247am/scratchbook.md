# Director's Scratchbook: The Phone Call at 2:47 AM (60s Viral Cut)
*Stick Figure Supernatural Horror Production Desk*

**Target System:** Google Flow (Veo 3.1 & Imagen 4)  
**Audio Architecture:** Audio recorded via Google AI Studio (Gemini 3.8 Flash TTS); video prompts contain **background score and diegetic foley ONLY**. Zero in-prompt voiceover/dialogue.  
**Visual Style DNA:** Minimalist 2D vector stick figure animation on a warm cream / off-white paper canvas background (`#F8F6F0`) revealed dynamically under lighting vectors, falling off into deep atmospheric pitch shadow (`#0A0B0E`) in unlit corners. Architectural elements and props are rendered in crisp, uniform black vector lines (`#0F172A`).  
**Subject DNA Standard (Strict Fingerless Vector Rule):** Stick figures are composed of pure, unbroken pencil-thin black vector line strokes that terminate in smooth rounded line caps (`stroke-linecap: round`). **Strictly zero individual fingers, zero human hands, zero knuckles, zero toes, and zero realistic fleshy joints.** Circular heads are **filled with solid opaque white (`#FFFFFF`)** and contoured with a **clean black vector contour (`#0F172A`)** matching the stroke weight of the limbs. Completely blank facial anatomy.  
**Total Production Runtime:** ~64 Seconds across **10 High-Retention Micro-Clips**.
