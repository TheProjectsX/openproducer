# Director's Scratchbook: The 9-5 Rat Race (Image-First Pipeline)

**Project Title:** The 9-5 Rat Race  
**Workflow Paradigm:** Image-First Video Production (Still Image Keyframe $\rightarrow$ Image-to-Video Motion)  
**Visual Style:** Stylized 2D Minimalist Animation (Stark pitch-black background, uniform pencil-thin white line art, geometric minimalist environments, Limbo aesthetic)  
**Pacing & Contrast:** Monotonous, repetitive, mechanical visual grind paired with an inspiring, deep motivational voiceover and swelling cinematic piano/strings.  
**Total Runtime:** ~13 Seconds across 2 Micro-Clips
