# Director's Scratchbook: The Uncanny Reflection (Stick Figure Horror)

**Project Title:** The Uncanny Reflection  
**Visual Paradigm:** Stylized 2D Minimalist Hand-Drawn Animation (Pitch-black cinematic noir void, stark uniform pencil-thin line weight, single cones of light, Limbo aesthetic)  
**Format:** 60-Second Short (10 Micro-Clips $\times$ 5s–7s)  
**Pacing Profile:** Dramatic Psychological Horror (Micro-beats, deliberate tension, 1 micro-action per clip)  
**Hard Constraints Enforced:**
1. *Closed-Lip / Blank Head Rule:* Stick figures have blank circular heads with zero mouth/facial features. Spoken whisper is strictly off-screen/supernatural audio; zero facial warping.
2. *Pre-Materialized & Prop-Free:* Pure linear vector limbs, no complex morphing props.
3. *Micro-Beat De-Cramming:* 1 camera move, 1 spatial focus, 1 unhurried motion per clip.
