[TITLE]
Someone Else Is in the Tower | 4K Animated Horror


[DESCRIPTION]
You have been stationed alone on a jagged Atlantic reef for months. Tonight, the front door rattles with three heavy knocks—and the wet footprints on the stone threshold only lead inside.

When the relief tender arrives days later under blinding sunlight, the tower is empty. The storm window on the top floor is shattered from the inside out, trailing seawater footprints across the floorboards. But the heavy iron hatch was locked from the inside... and someone just clicked the radio back on.
