# Motion Graphics & Kinetic Typography Cue Sheet

**Production ID:** `trading_sarcasm`  
**Editing Strategy:** Post-Production Overlay (CapCut / Premiere Pro / DaVinci Resolve)  
**Governing Rule:** Linter `L-05` (Anti-Subtitle & Non-Render Law) — AI footage is rendered 100% clean of burned-in text to prevent distorted gibberish and preserve maximum visual clarity. Kinetic floating text is layered on top in post.

---

## 1. Visual Typography Styling Guidelines

* **Font Style:** Bold, ultra-clean sans-serif (e.g., *Montserrat Black*, *The Bold Font*, or *Hind Siliguri Bold* for Bengali script).
* **Color Palette:**
  * **Primary Hook / Question:** Bright Yellow (`#FACC15`) with subtle black drop shadow and outline.
  * **Sarcastic Danger / Loss:** Luminous Crimson Red (`#EF4444`) with soft neon outer glow.
  * **Pacing / Animation:** Word-by-word bounce pop-in (`Scale 0% -> 110% -> 100% in 4 frames`), fast kinetic tracking.
* **Placement:** Central upper-third (Y: 25% – 35%), keeping clear of the character's mouth and eyes.
