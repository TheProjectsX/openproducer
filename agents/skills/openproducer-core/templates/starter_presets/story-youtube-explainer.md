# 📖 Story Template: High-Retention YouTube Explainer
> 3-Act educational retention arc. Strictly excludes video cinematography and episode plot.

## 🪝 Hook & Pacing Structure
- **0s – 6s (The Hook):** Counter-intuitive fact or provocative challenge to common intuition.
- **6s – 25s (The Hidden Problem):** Break down why the conventional answer is completely wrong.
- **25s – 50s (The Revelation / Mechanism):** Explain the core breakthrough or hidden mechanic step-by-step.
- **50s – 60s (The Takeaway & Call to Action):** Empowering conclusion that changes how the viewer sees the world.

## 🗣️ Dialogue & Vocal Rules
- **Sentence Cadence:** Active verbs, rhythmic progression, crystal-clear articulation.
- **TTS Expression Tags:** Inject `<breath>`, `<short pause>`, `<chuckle>` for natural presenter warmth.
- **Banned Cliches:** "Hey what's up guys", "Welcome back to another video", "Let's dive right in".
